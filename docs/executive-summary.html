HEMELVAL · WESTERN CAPE WINE

Hemelval

South African wine, discovered.

A mobile-first wine app built exclusively for South African wine — every estate, varietal, region, and festival of the Western Cape. The local competitor to Vivino, but SA-deep where Vivino is SA-thin.

THE PRODUCT

What it does, in one sentence.

Scan any SA wine bottle, log a structured tasting, build your palate profile, and get personalized recommendations — while giving Western Cape wine farms a verified, owned presence and demand analytics.

The name "Hemelval" (Afrikaans: "heaven-dew") evokes the Hemel-en-Aarde valley — South Africa's premium Pinot Noir/Chardonnay region. Domains hemelval.com + hemelval.co.za are available.
