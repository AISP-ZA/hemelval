## Why the freemium consumer model works (the data flywheel)

**The core insight:** a wine app's value compounds with data volume. Every rating, tasting note, and scan makes the recommendations better for everyone. A hard paywall strangles that flywheel at launch — you'd have a beautiful app with an empty database.

So the free tier must be **genuinely useful** (Vivino's lesson — users revolted when they paywalled core features in 2025). Decanta's free tier includes the full scan→rate→palate→cellar loop, because that's what generates the data. Pro gates the *advanced* value that engaged users will gladly pay for:

| Free (the data generators) | Pro R89/mo (the power features) |
|---|---|
| Scan / photo / barcode / QR | Unlimited scans (free tier: 10/mo) |
| Rate + structured tasting note | Advanced palate analytics (deep-dive charts) |
| Palate profile + basic Match % | Vintage comparison (same wine across years) |
| Cellar (owned bottles) | Ageability alerts + drinking-window push notifications |
| Basic recommendations | Ad-free experience |
| Events calendar | Early access to AI sommelier |
| Browse estates + wines | SA food-pairing wizard |

**Why R89/mo?** It's the price of one decent bottle of SA wine. The positioning writes itself: *"For less than a bottle a month, never drink a wine you don't love again."* R799/yr makes the annual equivalent ~2 months free — standard SaaS psychology.

## Why the Estate SaaS is the structural revenue

The consumer Pro tier is high-margin but volatile (churn). The Estate SaaS is **recurring, predictable B2B revenue** — the kind investors and a sustainable business are built on. Estates pay because Decanta gives them something they can't get elsewhere:

1. **A verified, owned presence** in the app every SA wine drinker uses (vs. Vivino, where their profile is generic and often wrong)
2. **Direct connection to engaged local consumers** — the people who actually buy their wines
3. **Anonymized regional palate analytics** — "demand for Pinotage is up 40% in Constantia this quarter" — intelligence that shapes what they plant and produce
4. **Event marketing** — list their tastings, harvest festivals, and wine-club openings in the calendar SA wine lovers check

| Tier | Price | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Small** | R499/mo | Single-estate, <20 wines | Verified badge, profile CMS, catalog, 1 event/mo |
| **Mid** | R999/mo | Multi-vineyard or active events | Everything + unlimited events, featured placement, analytics dashboard |
| **Large** | R1,499/mo | Portfolios, co-ops, wine-route bodies | Everything + analytics API, multi-estate management, sponsored shelf |

## Marketplace (Phase 3 — deferred)

Commission on wine sales (8–12%) is the highest-revenue ceiling but requires:
- Payment processing (PayFast: cards + Instant EFT + SnapScan/Zapper)
- Liquor licensing compliance per SA province
- Fulfilment (estate-direct shipping or partner retailers)
- Returns/cold-chain for MCC

Defer until the consumer + estate base is established. Launch marketplace only when it's a clear win for both sides.

## Why this beats Vivino's model in SA

| Vivino weakness | Decanta advantage |
|---|---|
| US/EU-centric; weak SA coverage | SA-deep from day one — every WO ward, Platter's/Veritas badges, local estates |
| Generic palate (no WSET rigor) | WSET-structured tasting that connoisseurs respect |
| B2B tools are generic globally | Estate analytics localized to SA regions + varietals |
| No SA payment/merchants | PayFast + EFT + SnapScan built in |
| No SA events/festivals | Wine Town, Wacky Wine, Swartland Revolution integrated |
| Ratings skew to international palates | Local critic overlay (Platter's, Tim Atkin) + SA-palate weighting |

## Revenue projection (illustrative, year 1)

Assuming 5,000 active free users + 8% Pro conversion (industry benchmark) + 30 estates:
- Consumer Pro: 400 × R89 × 12 = **~R427k/yr**
- Estate SaaS (avg R800/mo blended): 30 × R800 × 12 = **~R288k/yr**
- **Year 1 total: ~R715k** (before marketplace)
- Marketplace (Phase 3, year 2+): multiplies this 3–5× if executed well

The model is designed to be **cash-flow positive on consumer + estate alone**, with marketplace as upside.
