HOW IT WORKS

Scan. Taste. Discover.

Three steps to a smarter palate — built on the WSET systematic approach to tasting, the Noble Wine Aroma Wheel, and a database of every Western Cape wine region.

📷

Scan the bottle

Photograph the label, scan the barcode, or scan a QR code. Decanta identifies the wine from its SA database and shows ratings, awards, and pairings.

🍷

Log a proper tasting

Rate by the criteria connoisseurs use: nose, palate, tannins, acidity, finish, value — drawn from the WSET vocabulary, not a 5-star afterthought.

✨

Refine your palate

Every tasting sharpens your palate profile. Decanta recommends wines you'll love — matched to your taste, not a generic average.
